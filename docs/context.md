A small community studio runs classes with limited places. Today they take bookings over Instagram DMs and a shared spreadsheet. It goes wrong often: classes get overbooked, people turn up to sessions that already happened, and the owner spends Sunday nights reconciling.
The studio wants a simple web app where members can see upcoming classes and book a place. The popular classes are a problem. When the week's timetable opens, the evening classes fill within minutes, mostly from phones.
What the owner told us:
"If the app says you're booked, you're booked. I can't have two people in one spot."
"People should be able to see straight away if a class is full."
"I need to trust it more than I trust the spreadsheet."
That's all we know. Anything else, such as accounts, cancellations, waitlists or admin screens, is your call. Decide what matters most for a first version, build that well, and tell us what you assumed and what you left out.
